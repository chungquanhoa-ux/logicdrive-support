Privacy Policy – Logic Drive: Conveyor Jam

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Logic Drive: Conveyor Jam ("the game", "we") handles information.

1. Information we collect

  • We do not require an account and we do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Game progress (level, coins, unlocked parking slots, daily-bonus streak) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads (for example, to unlock a parking slot or earn coins). Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can limit ad personalisation in iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising and Tracking.

3. In-game purchases

The game has no real-money purchases. Coins are earned by playing or by watching optional ads.

4. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help.

5. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. The only third party that receives data through the game is Google (for advertising, as described above). Local game progress is deleted when you delete the app.

6. Your choices

  • Watching ads is always optional.
  • You can reset the advertising identifier or turn off tracking in iOS settings at any time.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
